Why doesn't your stomach digest itself?
It's bathed in acid around pH one point five, strong enough to dissolve metal.
So what protects it?
A lining that rebuilds constantly.
Every few days, your stomach grows a brand new surface, cell by cell, and sheds the old one.
But when that rebuild slows, an ulcer, or too many painkillers, the acid reaches the tissue underneath.
And then it does what you'd expect.
It digests you.
